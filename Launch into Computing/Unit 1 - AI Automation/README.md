A complex reconciliation process done by a bank accountant on a daily basis can be simplified by adapting to AI-powered automation. To make a telegraphic transfer, a bank customer traditionally has to visit a branch and fill out a telegraphic transfer form. The bank teller will check the form and upload it into the back-office system. The back-office staff will check the details of the transfer to make sure

1.  The amount is within the remitter's transaction limits,

2. The source of funds is reasonable

3. The remitter's details are up to date.

If any of these details are wrong, the customer will be contacted to verify the information. If all the details are correct, the payment will be forwarded to the compliance team, which will check that

 4. The beneficiary name is not sanctioned and

 5. The beneficiary's country of residence is not listed in the sanctions list.

The payment will then be forwarded to the payment team to perform the double entries and charge the beneficiary Bank Nostro account. This whole process takes up to 3 working days without automation.

With AI-powered automation, the bank customer can perform the transfer directly on a mobile application form, and the AI algorithm will check the amount, source of funds, remitter's details, and compliance lists while handling double entries within seconds. This transformation radically cuts remittance time from 3 working days down to a few seconds, significantly reduces error rates, and cuts unnecessary paper and labor costs.

This seamless execution relies on the integration of multiple core computing disciplines:

·       Software engineering builds the user-facing mobile application forms, robust backend architectures, and database connectivity, ensuring the system communicates reliably with data centers holding customer and compliance records.

·       Artificial intelligence algorithms power the automated verification logic, screening amounts, source-of-funds, compliance and handling double-entry bookkeeping, while flagging unusual transactions.

·       Cyber security professionals secure the entire ecosystem by protecting sensitive banking data and customer credentials through encryption and verifications.

However, as much as these systems transform our banking experiences, they also introduce notable challenges. They reduce the number of people employed by banks, raising concerns over corporate social responsibility. They lack flexibility when facing changing circumstances because they rely strictly on historical training data; if that data contains historical biases, it risks systematically misclassifying or unfairly rejecting legitimate customers and transactions
